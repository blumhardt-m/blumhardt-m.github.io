# Michael Blumhardt — Independent Research

My work examines statistical diagnostics, uncertainty auditing, and validation of scientific inference pipelines using public datasets and reproducible computational methods. Projects span astronomy, genomics, collider physics, and methods for evaluating AI-assisted research.

## Current work

As of October 30, 2026, the managed portfolio’s two live projects are a black-hole X-ray binary spectroscopic outburst inventory and historical replay validation of research procedures. Both remain at prerequisite stages; this listing does not indicate that research execution has begun.

## Unpublished manuscripts

The following projects have accompanying unpublished manuscripts:

- **Collider observable identifiability:** nuisance-mechanism degeneracy in reduced observables using CMS Open Data.
- **Gravitational lens classification:** background-disjoint evaluation of convolutional neural network classifiers.
- **GWAS annotation drift:** sensitivity of GWAS pathway interpretation to annotation version.
- **Galactic Center orbit inference:** sensitivity of a pushforward estimand to weight versus support perturbations of a posterior sample.

These entries describe the subjects of the manuscripts and do not imply journal publication or independent validation.

## Terminated work

The Gaia DR3 quasar-parallax manuscript project was terminated following journal rejection and a subsequent forensic audit. Its repository is retained as a historical record, with a notice explaining the reproducibility failures and distinguishing the referee’s findings from the later local audit.

## Methods

The work emphasizes explicit research questions, traceable inputs, executable analyses, and checks that connect scientific claims to the computations supporting them. Project records distinguish exploratory work, prerequisite preparation, completed analyses, and terminated studies.
