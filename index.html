Selected Work

  • Annotation version as a hidden degree of freedom in GWAS pathway interpretation
    Quantifies how genome annotation evolution propagates through GWAS locus-to-gene mapping and pathway enrichment pipelines, demonstrating threshold amplification in enrichment analyses. (Target journal: Bioinformatics)
  • Background-Disjoint Cross-Validation Reveals Background-Dependent Errors in CNN Gravitational Lens Classifiers
    Methodological analysis showing that standard cross-validation protocols can inflate reported performance of gravitational-lens classifiers due to background-dependent leakage. (Target journal: MNRAS / A&A)
  • Gaia DR3 quasar parallax systematics
    Diagnostic analysis of spatially correlated parallax residuals in Gaia DR3 quasars with emphasis on robustness under catalog selection and null controls. (Under review, A&A)

Methodological Posture

My work focuses on identifying structural sources of error and uncertainty in large scientific data products. Analyses emphasize falsifiable diagnostics, conservative inference, and explicit calibration of statistical claims. Projects are designed to remain informative under null outcomes and to support downstream scientific use rather than broad interpretive conclusions.
