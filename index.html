Large scientific datasets, survey systematics, and statistical diagnostics

Research Focus

Research centers on statistical diagnostics, uncertainty auditing, and validation of large-scale scientific inference pipelines.

Across domains, these projects study how aggregated or compressed observables can appear stable while diverging when conditioned on latent variables tied to the data-generation process.

  • Validation and uncertainty diagnostics in large scientific datasets
  • Statistical diagnostics for catalog-scale and high-dimensional data
  • Selection functions and completeness analysis
  • Cross-dataset and cross-domain consistency checks
  • Reanalysis of public datasets using reproducible computational pipelines

Selected Work

  • Non-identifiability of Nuisance Mechanisms in Reduced Collider Observables
    Demonstrates that compression of collider events into reduced observables can produce practical degeneracy between distinct nuisance mechanisms. Inclusive missing transverse momentum distributions from CMS Open Data admit multiple nuisance explanations that are distinguishable only through conditional diagnostics.
    Under review, Journal of Instrumentation (JINST)
  • Background-Disjoint Cross-Validation Reveals Background-Dependent Errors in CNN Gravitational Lens Classifiers
    Methodological analysis showing that conventional cross-validation protocols can inflate reported performance of gravitational-lens classifiers due to background-dependent leakage.
    Under review, Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society (MNRAS)
  • Gaia DR3 Quasar Parallax Systematics
    Diagnostic analysis of spatially correlated parallax residuals in Gaia DR3 quasars with emphasis on robustness under catalog selection and null controls.
    Under review, Astronomy & Astrophysics (A&A)
  • Annotation Version as a Hidden Degree of Freedom in GWAS Pathway Interpretation
    Quantifies how genome annotation evolution propagates through GWAS locus-to-gene mapping and pathway enrichment pipelines, demonstrating threshold amplification in enrichment analyses.
    Under review, Bioinformatics

Methodological Posture

My work focuses on identifying structural sources of uncertainty and error in large scientific data products. Analyses emphasize falsifiable diagnostics, conservative statistical inference, and explicit calibration of claims. Projects are designed to remain informative under null outcomes and to support downstream scientific use rather than broad interpretive conclusions.
